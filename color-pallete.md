## General Tips for an Elegant UI

1. **Desaturate everything** – Professional = muted. Avoid neon or pure colours.
2. **Use contrast strategically** – Only the most important action gets the primary accent.
3. **Add a pinch of warmth** – Use a warm neutral (e.g. `#E8DDD0`) for <5% of UI (alerts, badges) to balance cool palettes.
4. **Accessibility** – Ensure text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (WebAIM Contrast Checker).
5. **Typography** – Pair with clean sans‑serifs like Inter, DM Sans, or Source Sans Pro.
